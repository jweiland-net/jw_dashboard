============
Editor Guide
============

:Setting: :confval:`enableWidgetEditorGuide`
:Size: medium
:Refresh button: no

Shows the :confval:`welcomeMessage` (sanitized HTML), the contact person
marked as :guilabel:`Primary Contact` and the agency contact card
(:confval:`agencyName`, :confval:`agencyEmail`, :confval:`agencyPhone`,
:confval:`agencyWebsite`, toggled by :confval:`showAgencyLogo`).
