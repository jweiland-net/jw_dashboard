================
Recently Changed
================

:Setting: :confval:`enableWidgetRecentChanges`
:Size: small
:Refresh button: yes

Timeline of the latest created or edited pages and content elements (live
workspace) with record icon, editor and relative time. The title opens the
record in the editing form, the arrow opens the page module.

Only records the current backend user may see are listed (table permissions
and page access). Deleted records are skipped.

Settings: :confval:`recentChangesCount`.
