.. _widget-storage:

=======
Storage
=======

:Setting: :confval:`enableWidgetStorage`
:Size: small
:Refresh button: no

Shows the total size of all files indexed in FAL and a stacked bar by file type
(images, documents, videos, audio, other) with a legend carrying size, share and
number of files. The category colors follow a fixed order, validated for color
vision deficiencies in light and dark mode.
