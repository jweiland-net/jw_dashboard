==========
TYPO3 News
==========

:Setting: :confval:`enableWidgetTypo3News`
:Size: large
:Refresh button: yes

Lists the latest entries of the official TYPO3 news feed with date and
teaser. Links open in a new window.

The widget is loaded asynchronously when no cached data exists.

Settings: :confval:`typo3NewsFeedUrl`, :confval:`newsItemCount`,
:confval:`newsCacheTtl`, :confval:`requestTimeout`.
